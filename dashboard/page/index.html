Every day, every number

Qualified means the bot tagged them for a closer call. Signal Pilot fit is the under-$3k route. Booked counts only calls that are still standing on a calendar. Cancelled is a call the bot booked that is no longer there, either cancelled in GoHighLevel or deleted outright by a rep, which is what Joel does when the lead turns out to be a member or already owns the spreads bot. Showed and no-showed only count calls that have already happened, so a booking in the future sits in Booked and nowhere else. Positive/neutral, Hostile and Opted out split the repliers three ways and never overlap: hostile is anyone the bot sent down its complaint exit, opted out is anyone who actually asked to stop. Replies from before the complaint exit went live were read and classified by hand, so every day is on the same footing.

Each list, and how much of it is worked

Juan does not tag pickups or bookings, so every number here is read from the call log itself: an outbound call placed by his GoHighLevel user against someone carrying that list’s tag. Dialled counts people he has rung at least once, Still to call is the rest of the list. Connected counts people where at least one of his calls lasted twenty seconds or more, which is the shortest a real conversation runs — anything under that is a ring-out or a hang-up. Booked counts people with a call still standing on any calendar, which is not proof that Juan booked it.
